A nerd for the written word

Hi, I’m Claudia, a narrative consultant and games researcher who helps studios, brands and independent creators tell the stories that matter most to them and their audiences.

I research how games shape our collective memories of the past, and I take pride in building a fun, creative process for all types of projects.

Whatever you’re building, whether it’s a traditional novel, a game narrative, a creative product, or your brand, I’m here to make it meaningful to the diverse audiences you want to reach.

Game research

Prism

Research Associate, Creative AF Games

An action sci-fi game about an android coming into her own power, inspired by 90s and 00s Black girlhood and Afrofuturism. I worked with the narrative team and creative director on character arcs and story, grounded in original research on Black girlhood, Afrofuturism and decolonialization. I assisted with gameplay studies, planned cultural focus groups for the target audience, and wrote the studio's brand voice guidelines.

Academic research

Research on video games with historical narratives from the Black American perspective, looking at how narrative and mechanics work together to build or reduce player autonomy.
